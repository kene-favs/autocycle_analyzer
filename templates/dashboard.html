FIRE = Enter Now

A FIRE signal means price is at the Order Block right now during a session. WATCH means the setup is forming but price hasn't arrived yet. SKIP means no setup.

🥇

Gold Scalper Tab

Live XAUUSD micro-scalp signals with exact Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit levels. These are fast trades — M1 to M5 timeframes. Entry, SL and TP are pre-calculated for you.

📰

News Intelligence

The AI reads the macro calendar and tells you the gold bias before you trade. BEARISH stance = avoid BUY signals. BULLISH stance = avoid SELL signals.

🤖

Bot Connect

On a bot plan? Go to the Bot tab, enter your MT5 details and your account auto-connects. The scalper trades for you automatically during London and NY sessions.
